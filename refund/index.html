Refund Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Scope

This Refund Policy applies to all subscription plans for the MerkleClaw desktop application — Solo, Pro, and Enforcement.

2. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

All subscription plans include a 14-day unconditional full refund guarantee from the date of your first payment.

Conditions

  • Refund request must be submitted within 14 calendar days of the first payment.
  • No reason required.
  • Refund issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

How to request: Email billing@merkleclaw.com with your account email and (optionally) a reason.

3. Monthly Plans

  • Cancellation: Cancel anytime from your account settings. You retain access for the remainder of the current billing period.
  • Refunds: After the 14-day guarantee period, monthly plans are not eligible for refunds. Cancellation stops future renewals only.
  • Data retention: Local data on your device is unaffected. Cloud account information is retained for 90 days after cancellation.

4. Annual Plans

TimeframeRefund Rule
Within 14 days of paymentFull refund (no questions asked)
15 days – 6 monthsProrated refund: annual total minus months used × monthly rate
After 6 monthsNo refund; access continues until expiry

Example: Solo annual plan at $948, 3 months used → Refund = $948 − (3 × $99) = $651

5. Enforcement Annual Contracts

Refund terms for Enforcement plans are defined in the individual service agreement. Typical provisions include:

  • Full refund if the contract is terminated within 30 days of signing.
  • After 30 days, refunds are prorated based on the remaining contract period.
  • Custom services already delivered (training, installation support) are non-refundable.

6. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds are not available in the following cases:

  • Account terminated due to a violation of the Terms of Service.
  • The applicable refund period has expired.
  • Invite code activated but no payment was made (beta / free-tier — no refund policy applies).

7. Disputes

If you disagree with a refund decision, please email billing@merkleclaw.com. We will respond within 5 business days.
